Cancel a subscription or free trial

Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Quit Hypnosis → Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid being charged.

Restore purchases

Open the App → Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete your data

Open the App → Settings → Delete account, or email us.
